The IGC provides next-generation sequencing and bioinformatics analysis support for the Salk research community. We pair each project with a dedicated bioinformatics analyst who works alongside the research group from experimental design through manuscript submission — across RNA-seq, ChIP-seq/ATAC-seq/CUT&RUN, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, whole-genome/exome variant calling, and more.

This organization hosts the analysis tools, scripts, and pipelines built and maintained by IGC staff. Most repositories here are mirrors of individual members' personal projects, brought together in one place so the whole group — and the labs we work with — can find and reuse them easily.

## 1. Documentation

- **[IGC_Wiki](https://github.com/gevensen95/IGC_Wiki)** — the group's internal bioinformatics wiki: sequencing platforms, compute infrastructure, and step-by-step analysis workflows across every data type we support.
- **[IGC_SOPs](https://github.com/Salk-IGC/IGC_SOPs)** — standard operating procedure vignettes and hands-on tutorials for common IGC analyses.

## 2. What We Do

- **Consultation** — sounding board for feasibility, experimental design, and choosing the right assay.
- **Basic Analyses** — standard QC, alignment, quantification, and differential analysis for RNA-seq, ChIP-seq/ATAC-seq/CUT&RUN, and single-cell RNA-seq.
- **Custom Analyses** — integrative multi-omic analyses, novel pipelines, and tool development for questions that don't fit an off-the-shelf workflow.
- **Data hosting & submission** — hosting datasets for collaborators, UCSC Genome Browser track generation, and GEO submission support.
- **Education/Training** — annual hands-on bioinformatics workshops and one-on-one training.
- **Scientific writing** — methods sections, figure legends, and manuscript/grant support.
